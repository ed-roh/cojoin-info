Support

For account help, bug reports, safety concerns or appeals, email edwardroh89@gmail.com. You can also use Settings → Help in the app.

Report or block

Open a vibe and choose Report vibe or block host. In a chat, use the safety menu or press and hold a message to report its sender. You can manage your block list in Settings.

Friend invitations

Saving a name or importing a contact creates a private saved contact. To connect accounts, send an invitation link by text or email, or scan a QR code. Each link works once. If you install the app after receiving an invite, reopen the original link.

Account deletion

Use Settings → Delete Account. If you cannot access your account, see account deletion help.
