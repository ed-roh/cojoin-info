Delete your Cojoin account

In the app, open Settings → Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. Deletion removes your account and associated app data. See the Privacy Policy for retention details.

If you cannot sign in, email edwardroh89@gmail.com from your account email address with the subject “Cojoin account deletion.” We will verify ownership before acting. Never send your password.

Deleting the app from your phone does not delete your account. Signing out preserves your account so you can sign back in.
